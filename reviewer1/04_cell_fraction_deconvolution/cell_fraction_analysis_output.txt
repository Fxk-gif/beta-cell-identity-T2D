==============================================================================
Conclusion
==============================================================================

  1. The beta-cell fraction of the T2D donor (human4) (37.99% of total cells) is not lower than the ND mean (29.46%),
     but higher; and the beta fraction differs greatly among the three ND donors (21.52% ~ 45.02%).
  2. Therefore, the cell-type annotations of the single-cell datasets do NOT support a decrease in beta-cell (compositional) fraction.
  3. The Baron T2D group has only 1 donor, so no statistical test is possible; the variation among the 3 ND donors
     far exceeds the ND-T2D difference, indicating that this variation is driven mainly by inter-donor/technical batch effects (islet isolation, cell sorting),
     rather than disease state.
  4. The core judgment in Reviewer 1 Comment #4 is correct: the absence of a single-cell PDX1/NKX6-1 signal cannot be
     interpreted as compositional depletion of mature beta-cells; dropout and low abundance are competing explanations,
     and the computed cell fractions provide no evidence of a decreased beta fraction.
  5. The appropriate action is therefore to withdraw/qualify the compositional causal claim of 'compositional depletion',
     and to re-attribute the bulk decrease in PDX1/NKX6-1 to technical/confounding factors that cannot be excluded, rather than reduced mature beta-cells.
